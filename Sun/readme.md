# Solar Models and Solar Evolution

A series of plots model Sun data from Bahcall and Pinsonneault 2004 (BP2004).

Plots
 * BP2004 Solar Model - comparison of present-day Sun with n=3 polytrope and uniform density sphere models
 * MS_Sun_energy - energy generation evolution of the Sun from birth to ZAMS
 * Solar Interiod Composition - present-day composition as a function of mass fraction (radius): H, He, C, N, and O
 * <sup>3</sup>He fraction in the present-day Sun
 * Solar Neutrino Spectrum
