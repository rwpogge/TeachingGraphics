# Spaceflight Graphics

Work in progress.

## Contents

### Voyager Trajectories

Trajectories of Voyager 1 and 2 from the JPL Horizons database.  View looking down on the 
ecliptic and from the side (Voyager 1 is going way out of plane after the Saturn flyby).

### Voyager 2 Gravity Assist

A series of graphics for teaching about gravity assist orbits. This one is for 
the Voyager 2 Saturn encounter which provided a gravity assist to send Voyager 2
to Uranus.
